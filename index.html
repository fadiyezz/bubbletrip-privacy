BUBBLE TRIP · GAME FOR ANDROID & iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Bubble Trip: Fruit Kick Shooter ("the game") is a casual bubble-shooter game for families by Studio Ekin. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (stars earned per level) and your sound setting are saved only on your device. The game has no user accounts, no chat, no sign-in and no in-game purchases, and the game itself does not send this information anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows occasional full-screen ads between games (never during a level) using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Because the game is played by children as well as adults, every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment and limited to G-rated content, and users are treated as under the age of consent. This means Google serves only non-personalised ads and does not use the data below for interest-based advertising or remarketing. To serve ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may process:

  • Device information, such as device model and operating system
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data)

The game does not ask for permission to track you on iOS. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's policies for child-directed apps.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the game uses Google's consent tool. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads. On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The game is designed for families and may be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the game has no accounts, no chat, no sharing, and no way to enter personal details. Ads are child-directed and non-personalised as described above. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — to load ads.
  • VIBRATE (Android) — for short vibration feedback when a kick lands.
